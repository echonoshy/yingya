# Narrated explanation

Use within the existing plan and production workflow. A manuscript provides
content; choose visible subjects and actions that explain it. Build a reason to
watch, a causal or conceptual progression, concrete examples and an ending suited
to the user's goal. Do not append a promotional CTA to every explanation.

Choose images, supplied footage, objects, editable diagrams or typography by the
idea being explained. Avoid repeated paragraph cards and one generated image per
sentence. Preserve an explicitly requested slide/typographic treatment. A faceless
brief excludes presenter shots; otherwise do not impose that restriction.

Use stable scene IDs and the approved visual system. New narration uses the
saved voice and measured audio; visual-only revisions reuse it. Read
[production.md](production.md) for dependency order and
[remotion-authoring.md](remotion-authoring.md) for scene implementation.
Sources live under `src/`, media under `assets/`, exports under `renders/`,
reports under `.yingya/reports/`; each delivered version freezes all dependencies.
