# Information structures in time

Choose from the actual relationship in the material. These are useful patterns,
not an exhaustive catalog or a fixed shot count.

| Relationship | Composition | Motion that explains it | Final readable state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sequence | One path, stages along the reading direction | Introduce each stage, then extend its connection as that step is explained | Whole sequence, with outcome clearly distinguished |
| Comparison | Same baseline, units and visual scale; aligned corresponding attributes | Establish the shared context, reveal each alternative, then emphasize the actual difference | Both sides and their source/units remain visible |
| Hierarchy | Nested groups or stacked levels matching the real containment/order | Show context, focus a level, then reconnect it to the whole | Understandable parent/child relationships |
| Breakdown | A recognizable whole with its parts and labels | Separate one meaningful part or layer at a time, explain its role, then show how it connects | Whole-to-part relationship is still apparent |

Read the corresponding upstream layout in [the index](index.md) if its spatial
organization would help. Reference examples are not evidence for the user's
subject. Do not invent quantities, layers or steps to fit a layout.

## Select an implementation

The installed `flow-path` can cover short linear sequences, `number-compare`
two supported metrics, and `beam-network` branching/converging connections.
Read their actual catalog options before use. A hierarchy, detailed chart or
exploded mechanism may require custom HTML/SVG. A card grid is not a substitute
for containment, scale or causality. Real recorded actions keep their footage;
annotated diagrams can supplement them when the story needs explanation.

Text and numbers stay independently editable. Derive chart geometry from the
same data used in its labels; preserve source, units and time range. Keep a
common scale for comparable quantities, and label any intentional axis break.
For example, sample values 40 and 70 cannot appear as equal bars. Mark synthetic
acceptance data explicitly rather than presenting it as a business result.

## Plan and time the explanation

Use existing scene fields. `visualDirection` describes the relationship and
composition; `motion` describes reveal, change and hold; `assetStrategy` names
real media versus editable elements. `recipe` belongs to the footage assembler
and is not a field for a style or information-structure name.

For “资料 → 整理 → 结论”, a useful motion note is: “先出现资料，讲到整理时连接到
第二阶段，结论出现后保留完整路径。” This is more actionable than “高级动画”.
During planning, durations remain estimates. Resolve reveal times against
measured narration, observed source actions or intentional silent reading time
before writing the final timeline. A selected animation duration cannot shorten
speech or force locked content out of a hard duration limit.

For portrait, a vertical progression or stacked comparison may read better than
a reduced landscape diagram. Recompose at the target dimensions while keeping
the same factual relationships, legible text and caption space. Within one
export the composition has fixed dimensions; device viewing scales the result.
